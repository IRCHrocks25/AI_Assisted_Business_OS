Who is AI KONIK built for?
AI KONIK is built for businesses between $1M and $20M in revenue — companies that have outgrown manual systems and generic AI tools, but aren't ready for a multi-million-dollar enterprise rollout. If your team is coordinating complex workflows across multiple stakeholders, you're in the right band.
How is AI KONIK different from a generic AI chatbot?
A chatbot waits for you to figure out what to ask. AI KONIK ships with specialized agents that already know the work — drafting contracts, reviewing claims, analyzing freight data, generating clinical notes — and a tested prompt library organized by industry and use case. You start with a system, not a blank window.
Is our data secure? Are you compliant with our regulations?
Yes. AI KONIK is built on a compliance-ready architecture that supports HIPAA, SOC 2, and GDPR natively. Sensitive workflows — patient records, M&A documents, financial filings — operate inside the same secure environment your auditors are already comfortable with.
Which industries do you support?
Nine industry verticals today: Legal, Healthcare, Finance, Accounting, Marketing, Real Estate, Technology, Logistics, and Manufacturing. Each one ships with its own agents, prompt collections, and compliance configurations.
We're not great at writing AI prompts. Will that be a problem?
No. The platform includes a curated library of more than 60,000 tested prompts, organized by industry and use case. Your team can use them directly, save them to collections, modify them for your workflows, or contribute your own as you go.
What exactly is the 6-Sprint Delivery Method?
It's the structured engagement that comes with the platform. Six sprints — Map, Structure, Integrate, Deploy, Train, Optimize — led by consultants who already work in your industry. They take you from first conversation to a fully running system, then keep tuning it as you scale.
What does AI KONIK look like for a Legal or Healthcare team specifically?
For a legal team: agents for M&A due diligence, contract review, IP research, and compliance monitoring, with prompts written for legal language and risk patterns. For a healthcare team: agents for clinical documentation, billing automation, and patient communication, with workflows that stay HIPAA-compliant from the first step. The pattern repeats across every supported vertical.
Can our team learn AI through the platform?
Yes. AI KONIK includes live workshops and masterclasses from practitioners across 40+ industries, plus a curated AI Tool Directory with honest community reviews. The platform is built as much for upskilling as for execution.
What does it cost to start?
You can start free, no credit card required. Your first agent runs in minutes. From there, you can scale into a full deployment when you're ready, with the 6-Sprint engagement bringing your team and your workflows fully onto the platform.
