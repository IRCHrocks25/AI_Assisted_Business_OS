Not what a base model guessed.

What you walk away with: Production RAG system grounded in your data, with accurate, cited, explainable AI responses — deployed and integrated.

Senior engineers. Every engagement is led by a senior architect who designs the system and a senior engineering lead who builds it. We don't use offshore delivery pods. You'll work with the same small team from discovery through deployment — and you'll know them by name.
Have you actually deployed AI systems at enterprise scale?

Architecture Philosophy

We build AI systems that follow modern production principles: event-driven where possible, observable by default, containerized and horizontally scalable, secure by design.

Every system we build has: structured logging, distributed tracing, health checks, failure recovery, cost monitoring (critical for LLM systems), and versioned deployments. If it's running in production, you can see what it's doing — and why.

Security Model

  • SOC 2 Type II in progress (audit timeline available on request)
  • Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Role-based access control with granular scope enforcement
  • Comprehensive audit logging (every action, actor, timestamp)
  • Data handling: GDPR-aligned, right-to-erasure, data residency options
  • LLM data handling: self-hosted open-source models for data governance when needed

Reference Process

On the discovery call, we discuss relevant past engagements. Where appropriate and with client consent, we introduce you to reference clients — typically engineering leaders from organizations similar in scale and complexity to yours. This happens before any major commitment, not as a post-sale formality.
