What if my business doesn't fit a standard template?
There is no standard template. We configure the OS around YOUR workflow — your clients, your industry, your tools. The discovery call is where we map exactly how it runs for you.
Will the AI emails actually sound like me?
Yes. We train the system on your past emails, your writing samples, and your brand voice guidelines. Most clients tell us they can't tell which drafts came from the AI — and neither can their customers.
Do I still control what goes out?
Always. Every email, every post, every follow-up can be reviewed and approved before it sends. You can also set autopilot rules for low-risk actions (like standard confirmations) so routine tasks don't need your attention at all. Your call.
I already have a CRM, a chatbot, and an email tool. Will this replace them?
Usually not. The OS sits above your existing tools and orchestrates them — your CRM still stores contacts, your email tool still sends mail. We don't ask you to rip and replace. We ask you to let us connect what you already have.
What if I get stuck or something breaks?
You have direct access to our team during setup and the first 30 days. After that, ongoing support is included — no ticket queues, no level-one bots. If something breaks, one of our engineers fixes it.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on your business size, your stack, and which engines you need to prioritize. We discuss it transparently on the discovery call — no surprise bills, no hidden tiers. You'll walk away with a clear number, whether you move forward or not.

The architecture

The OS is built in n8n (our orchestration layer), integrated with OpenAI, Anthropic, and specialized models for voice and transcription. Each engine runs as an independent workflow with shared context — so they can feed each other without becoming a monolith.

Everything is hosted on enterprise-grade infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance, encrypted data at rest, and full audit logs.

What we're NOT

This isn't a CRM (we integrate with yours). It isn't a marketing tool (we automate yours). It isn't a chatbot (we build full multi-channel systems). It isn't a Zapier template (we architect custom workflows).

If you want a tool you manage, buy a tool. If you want a system that runs itself, we build that.
